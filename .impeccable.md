## Design Context

### Users
- **Admin Console**: Platform operators — one or two people managing the entire marketplace. They know the system well, work efficiently, make consequential decisions (approving artisans, QC-ing products, managing payouts). Context: desktop browser, focused sessions.
- **Artisan Portal**: African artisans and craftspeople selling their work. Varying tech literacy. Pride in their craft. Excited to have a professional platform for their work. Context: mix of mobile and desktop.

### Brand Personality
Crafts Continent is premium African artisanal commerce. Three words: **sovereign, rooted, refined**.
- Not "exotic" or "tribal" — that's tourist-gaze
- Not generic e-commerce — this has cultural depth and pride
- Not cold tech — warm, craft-forward, human

### Aesthetic Direction
**Core palette (from globals.css):**
- Canvas: #0C0A09 (near-black, warm)
- Elevated: #1C1917
- Surface: #292524
- Gold: #D4A853 (primary accent)
- Hunter green: #355E3B (brand secondary)
- Saddle brown: #8B4513 (warm tertiary)
- Off-white: #FAF9F6
- Terracotta: #C4704B

**Theme**: Dark luxury. The main site is already dark (#0C0A09 base). Portals extend this into their own distinct zones.

**Portal differentiation:**
- Admin Console: Authoritative. Precise. The operations hub. Geometric, sharp, efficient.
- Artisan Portal: Empowering. Warm. A creative home. Organic within structure.

**References that capture the right feel:**
- Shopify Admin (functional authority) but with African aesthetic warmth
- For artisan: Etsy Studio meets a premium African gallery

### Design Principles
1. **Portals ≠ Storefront** — different visual grammar from the consumer site, same brand DNA
2. **Gold is sacred** — #D4A853 is used sparingly and always earns its place
3. **Warmth through texture** — CSS patterns inspired by African geometric textiles, not clipart
4. **Typography does the heavy lifting** — distinctive fonts for each portal replace the need for complex illustration
5. **Empty states teach** — every zero-state on dashboards should orient, not just say "nothing here"

### Font Pairs (portal pages only — don't override site-wide fonts)
- **Admin Console**: Bricolage Grotesque (display) + Hanken Grotesk (body)
- **Artisan Portal**: Piazzolla (display) + Hanken Grotesk (body)
Both via Google Fonts, loaded only on portal login pages.
